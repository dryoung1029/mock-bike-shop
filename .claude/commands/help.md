# /help

Say this, adjusted to what's already done (check `docs/PROGRESS.md` if it exists):

> Here's what I can do for your website. Type any of these:
>
> - **/setup** — set up the site with me, step by step. Start here. It's a
>   demo right now; we'll swap the sample info for your real info as we go.
> - **/article** — write a new blog post in your voice. I research it, cite
>   real sources, and you approve it before it goes live.
> - **/publish** — put an approved post live.
> - **/photos** — add photos of the shop and the team. You upload through one
>   link; I do the rest.
> - **/competitors** — see how other bike shops' websites near you compare and
>   where you can beat them.
> - **/reddit-ideas** — find the questions riders are really asking about
>   repairs, tune-ups, and e-bikes, and turn them into article ideas.
> - **/audit** — find weak spots on the site and fix the easy ones.
> - **/traffic** — read your Google Search Console numbers and tell you what to do.
> - **/benchmark** — score the site against a quality checklist and against
>   the shop websites you want to beat.
> - **/launch** — point your real web address at the site (with a tech-savvy
>   helper on the call).
>
> Or just tell me what you want in normal words — "change Saturday hours to
> 10–4", "add Sam's photo", "a basic tune-up is $85 now, not $79" — and I'll
> do it and show you.
