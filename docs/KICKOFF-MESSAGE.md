# What to send the owner

Send Jason `docs/START-HERE.md` (or the link to it on GitHub) and this note:

---

Jason — here's the J's Wheels demo website, ready for you to set up with Claude.

Open this and follow the four steps: **[link to START-HERE.md]**

Short version: make a free GitHub account, get access to the site's files,
open them in Claude Code, and type `/setup`. Claude will ask you questions —
hours, repair prices, who's on the team, how you'd describe the shop — one at
a time, and tell you exactly what to click when a step needs a website like
Cloudflare.

Right now everything on the site is sample data (a made-up address, phone, and
prices). Nothing goes on a real domain until you say so, and that last step is
done together with a helper on the call.

Plan on three or four short sessions. Send a message if you get stuck.
