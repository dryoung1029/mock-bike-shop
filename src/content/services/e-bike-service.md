E-bikes are heavier and faster than regular bikes, so brakes, tires, and chains wear out sooner. Regular service keeps them running smoothly.

## What's included

- Everything in a standard tune-up, with e-bike-rated parts.
- Brake pads and rotors checked for wear.
- Chain and cassette wear measured.
- Drive system check and firmware update where the manufacturer allows it.
- Battery connections and charge port inspected.

## What we won't do

We don't unlock speed limiters or open up battery packs. If a battery looks damaged, we'll help you contact the manufacturer.
