Most riding discomfort comes from a bike that doesn't fit its rider. A fitting fixes that by adjusting the bike to you.

## How it works

1. **We talk.** How you ride, how far, and what hurts or feels off.
2. **We watch you ride** on a trainer and measure the key angles.
3. **We adjust:** saddle height and setback, handlebar reach and height, and cleat position.
4. **We write it down** so your settings can be copied to your next bike.

Fittings are by appointment so there's no rush.
