An overhaul is the deepest service we offer. We strip the bike down to the frame, clean every part, check it for wear, and rebuild it with fresh grease and new cables.

## What's included

- Everything in a standard tune-up.
- Drivetrain removed and deep-cleaned: chain, cassette, chainrings, derailleurs.
- Headset, bottom bracket, and hubs cleaned and re-greased (or replaced if worn).
- New brake and shift cables and housing.
- Frame cleaned and inspected.

## Before we start

We look the bike over with you and write down what it needs. You approve the estimate before any work begins.
