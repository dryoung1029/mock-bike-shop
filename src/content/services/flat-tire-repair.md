Flats happen. Bring the bike (or just the wheel) in and we'll usually have you back on the road before you've finished a coffee.

## What we do

- Replace the tube (or reseal a tubeless setup).
- Run a hand around the inside of the tire to find the thorn, glass, or wire that caused it.
- Check the rim strip and tire wear so the next flat isn't tomorrow.
- Pump the tire to the right pressure for you and your riding.

## Want to learn to do it yourself?

Ask while you're here. We're happy to show you how — it's a five-minute skill that saves a long walk home.
