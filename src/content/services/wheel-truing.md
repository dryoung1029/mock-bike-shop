A true wheel rolls straighter, brakes more evenly, and lasts longer. Wheel work is one of the things a good shop does best by hand.

## What we do

- **Truing:** straighten side-to-side wobble and up-and-down hop.
- **Spoke replacement:** swap broken spokes and re-tension the wheel.
- **Hub service:** clean and adjust or replace the bearings.
- **Custom builds:** hand-built wheels for touring, commuting, gravel, or racing.
