A tune-up is the service most bikes need most often. We put your bike in the stand, go over it from front to back, and fix the small things before they turn into big ones.

## What's included

- **Brakes** adjusted, pads checked, cables or hoses inspected.
- **Shifting** indexed so every gear clicks in cleanly.
- **Wheels** checked for true and loose spokes; tires checked for wear and inflated.
- **Bolts** torqued on the stem, bars, seatpost, and crank.
- **A test ride** and a short note telling you anything we'd keep an eye on.

A **standard tune-up** also includes a full drivetrain clean and wheel truing.

## Parts

If something needs replacing — a worn chain, brake pads, a cable — we call you with the price first. Nothing gets added to your bill without a yes.
